# FreeBizMindMap — 免费开源的商业架构无限画布思维导图

这是一套自用定制、超高自由度商业架构无限画布思维导图模板。

摆脱传统大厂思维导图软件死板固定布局的束缚，支持上下左右任意拖拽、缩放，任何人都可以按照自己的习惯个性化定制画布内容。

内置彩色动态流动箭头，直观展示业务与管理流转关系：从公司总部 → 中层管理 → 基层岗位，清晰体现管控传导逻辑，流向一目了然。

适合搭建企业组织架构、部门 SOP、业务规划蓝图，也可用于个人思路脑暴。开箱即用，终身免费，无会员付费门槛。

## English

This is a self-customized infinite-canvas mind map template for business architecture. Break free from the rigid layout limits of mainstream mind map tools. You can drag and zoom in all directions, and fully personalize the canvas according to your own habits.

It comes with colorful animated flowing arrows, which visually display the management and business transfer path from headquarters to middle management and frontline staff.

Suitable for enterprise organization charts, department SOPs, business blueprint planning and personal brainstorming. Ready to use, 100% free forever, no paid membership required.

> Note: The video above shows the animated flowing arrow effects. Open the HTML file to experience the full interactive canvas.
